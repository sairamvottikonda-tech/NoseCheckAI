Photo Guide

Take a frontal photo with good, even lighting and your nose clearly visible.

Face the camera directly
Turns beyond 3° can't be measured reliably
Use even, natural lighting
Avoid strong side shadows
Keep a neutral expression
Smiling can shift nostril symmetry
Hold the phone still, arm's length away
Closer shots distort the measurement
